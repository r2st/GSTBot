### Thread 2 — 5 GST mistakes (educational + CTA)

**1/**
5 things every Indian business owner gets wrong about GST. 🧵

The last one costs people real money every year.

**2/**
**Mistake 1: Not checking a supplier's GSTIN.**

If your supplier's GSTIN is wrong or mistyped on an invoice, your input tax credit (ITC) can be rejected.

Check it before you pay: https://gst.doaide.com/lookup (free, no login)

**3/**
**Mistake 2: Using the wrong GST rate.**

Same-looking products can have different rates. Rate depends on the HSN/SAC code, not on what you call the item.

Look up the code and rate here: https://gst.doaide.com/hsn

**4/**
**Mistake 3: Mixing up CGST+SGST and IGST.**

Within the same state → CGST + SGST (split equally).
Between two states → IGST.

Wrong split = wrong invoice = headaches at filing time.

Quick calculator: https://gst.doaide.com/calculator

**5/**
**Mistake 4: Filing GSTR-3B without matching GSTR-2B.**

If your purchase records don't match what your suppliers filed, you may claim ITC you aren't eligible for.

Always compare before you file.

**6/**
**Mistake 5: Assuming "I'll sort it out at the end of the quarter."**

Mismatches found late are expensive. Mismatches found early are a quick fix.

Upload your invoices to DoAide GST and we flag them as you go. Free for 50 invoices/month.

👉 https://gst.doaide.com
